PlaceOpt

Standard-cell placement as combinatorial optimization. A C++17 simulated-annealing placer and an analytical flow (quadratic global placement, then legalization, then detailed placement) run on netlists synthesized by Yosys onto real SkyWater SKY130 HD cells, with cell footprints and pin locations taken from the LEF files. On small instances, results are checked against exact optima from OR-Tools CP-SAT.

Placement viewer

Cells are drawn at their legal row and site positions, colored by cell function. Thin lines are the nets of the 120 longest connections. Use the slider to step through a simulated-annealing run as the temperature drops. The objective is HPWL only, with no density or congestion term, so the analytical flow packs cells toward the input pins on the left edge. A production placer would spread them for routability.

Flow comparison

Median HPWL over 5 seeds (lower is better) and median runtime on a 2-core cloud VM. Utilization is 60%, inputs sit on the left die edge and outputs on the right, and clock nets are excluded because clock-tree synthesis routes them.

Annealing schedule

VPR-style adaptive schedule: the cooling rate depends on the acceptance ratio, and the move-range window shrinks to keep acceptance near 44%.

Optimality gap vs. CP-SAT

For small netlists, CP-SAT proves the minimum-HPWL placement on the same grid, with the same pin offsets and terminals. The gap shows how far simulated annealing (best and median of 10 seeds) lands from the true optimum.

Built with Yosys 0.33, the SkyWater SKY130 HD standard-cell LEFs (Apache-2.0), OR-Tools CP-SAT, pybind11 and Chart.js. Source: github.com/Nags-gk/placeopt.
